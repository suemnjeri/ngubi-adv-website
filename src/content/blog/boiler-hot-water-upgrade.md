We have some exciting news! 

If you have stayed with us at the forest camp before, you know there is nothing quite like waking up to the morning fog rolling through the trees, the crisp highland air, and the incredible chorus of forest birds. But we also know that a chilly morning is always better when followed by a steaming hot shower.

To elevate your outdoor experience, we have officially completed construction and testing on our new **high-capacity eco-friendly water heating system**. 

## What This Upgrade Means for Your Next Stay
* **Consistent Hot Water:** You can count on reliable hot water at the ablution blocks.
* **Eco-Friendly Design:** Built to integrate cleanly with our forest conservation principles, maximizing heating efficiency while minimizing environmental impact.
* **Large Group Ready:** Optimized to handle peak morning hours for youth groups, corporate teams, and large camping parties simultaneously.

## Field Notes: The Science of Staying Warm in the Forest
Did you know that the forest canopy retains moisture, which makes morning temperatures feel slightly crisper than in open clearings? When preparing for a trip out here, a hot shower  helps stabilize your core body temperature before the day's activities begin.
